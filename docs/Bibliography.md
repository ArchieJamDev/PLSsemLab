# PLSsemLab Bibliography

**Complete APA 7th edition reference list of every source cited by
PLSsemLab.**

Inside jamovi, each method-backed table will cite its own sources in
jamovi's native numbered format (`jamovi/00refs.yaml` + `refs:`). jamovi's
reference renderer fixes that format and only shows it under `Table`
results; it is a constraint of jamovi's interface, not a choice of the
module. Where the report text names a source inline (e.g. "Henseler et al.,
2015"), it says "see references" instead of a bracket number, because jamovi
assigns the number at render time. This page is the authoritative APA 7th
edition list (CODE_STYLE.md §21): every key in `jamovi/00refs.yaml` must
appear here, and every source cited in the report text must appear in
`jamovi/00refs.yaml`.

**Design-stage status (2026-11-10).** Every DOI below was found on the
publisher's or a bibliographic database's page on 2026-11-10. Volume, issue
and page numbers must still be checked against each DOI before the first
release, as was done for EGAnetLab and MokkenLab.

# Bibliografía de PLSsemLab

**Lista completa, en formato APA 7.ª edición, de todas las fuentes que cita
PLSsemLab.**

Dentro de jamovi, cada tabla respaldada por un método citará sus propias
fuentes en el formato numerado nativo de jamovi (`jamovi/00refs.yaml` +
`refs:`). Esta página es la lista autorizada en APA 7.ª edición
(CODE_STYLE.md §21).

**Estado en fase de diseño (2026-11-10).** Cada DOI de abajo se encontró en
la página del editor o de una base bibliográfica el 2026-11-10. Antes de la
primera publicación hay que comprobar volumen, número y páginas contra cada
DOI, como se hizo en EGAnetLab y MokkenLab.

## Software

Rademaker, M. E., & Schuberth, F. (2020). *cSEM: Composite-based structural
equation modeling* [R package]. https://floschuberth.github.io/cSEM/

## PLS-SEM: method and reporting

Hair, J. F., Jr., Hult, G. T. M., Ringle, C. M., Sarstedt, M., Danks, N. P.,
& Ray, S. (2021). *Partial least squares structural equation modeling
(PLS-SEM) using R: A workbook*. Springer.
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-80519-7

Hair, J. F., Risher, J. J., Sarstedt, M., & Ringle, C. M. (2019). When to use
and how to report the results of PLS-SEM. *European Business Review, 31*(1),
2–24. https://doi.org/10.1108/EBR-11-2018-0203

Tenenhaus, M., Esposito Vinzi, V., Chatelin, Y.-M., & Lauro, C. (2005). PLS
path modeling. *Computational Statistics & Data Analysis, 48*(1), 159–205.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.csda.2004.03.005

## Higher-order constructs

Sarstedt, M., Hair, J. F., Cheah, J.-H., Becker, J.-M., & Ringle, C. M.
(2019). How to specify, estimate, and validate higher-order constructs in
PLS-SEM. *Australasian Marketing Journal, 27*(3), 197–211.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ausmj.2019.05.003 — the two-stage approaches the
module follows (disjoint two-stage).

## Moderation

Kenny, D. A. (2018, September 15). *Moderation*.
https://davidakenny.net/cm/moderation.htm — proposes f² of 0.005, 0.01 and
0.025 as small, medium and large interaction effects (used by Hair et al.,
2021, ch. 8).

Henseler, J., Ringle, C. M., & Sarstedt, M. (2015). A new criterion for
